Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Tahir is a prayer companion app for iPhone. It's built so your worship stays between you and Allah: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. This policy explains the little information that does leave your phone, and why.

What stays on your phone

  • Your prayer and Tahajjud logs, streaks and statistics
  • Your settings, including your prayer time calculation method and reminder choices
  • Your Quran reading progress
  • Saved places for Prayer Lock, if you use it

This information is stored only on your device. We have no servers and no copy of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

Tahir uses your location to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction.

  • Prayer times: your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to the Aladhan prayer times service, together with your chosen calculation method. No name, account or device identifier is sent with it. Aladhan's own privacy practices apply to that request.
  • Qibla: worked out on your phone. Nothing is sent.
  • Prayer Lock places: if you add safe places (such as home or work), your phone checks whether you're at one of them. This happens entirely on your device.

You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings. Tahir will then use the last location it knew, or a default city.

Prayer Lock and Screen Time

Prayer Lock uses Apple's Screen Time framework to pause the apps you choose during prayer times. Apple handles your app choices and usage on your device. Tahir can't see which apps you use or how you use them, and none of it leaves your phone.

Notifications

Prayer, follow-up and Tahajjud reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Quran verses

Some verse translations are downloaded from the AlQuran Cloud service. These requests only ask for verse text and contain no information about you.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, Tahir opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you submit there, including an email address if you add one, is shared with us through Google Forms and used only to improve the app. Google's privacy policy applies to the form.

Children

Tahir has no accounts and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@tahir5dailyprayers.com.
