Common questions

My prayer times are different from my mosque's

Mosques use different calculation methods, especially for Fajr and Isha. By default Tahir uses the Tahir standard, which follows the timings on alislam.org. To match your mosque, go to More › Prayer Times and choose its method (for example Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura or Karachi), or set your own. You can also switch between standard and Hanafi Asr there.

I'm not getting prayer reminders
  • Turn reminders on in More › Namaz Reminders.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Tahir in iOS Settings › Notifications › Tahir.
  • Leave Background App Refresh on for Tahir (iOS Settings › Apps › Tahir), so reminders keep being topped up even if you don't open the app.
  • Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can silence reminders. Add Tahir to your allowed apps if you want them to come through.
How do I change the adhan or turn it off for a prayer?

Go to More › Namaz Reminders and choose the adhan, a soft chime or off for each prayer. Tap Play the adhan to hear it. The adhan follows your ringer volume and the Ring/Silent switch.

I wear an Apple Watch and don't hear the adhan

While your iPhone is locked, iOS sends Tahir's reminders to your watch, and the watch only taps: it can't play the adhan. To hear the adhan on your iPhone, open the Watch app on your iPhone and turn off Notifications › Tahir. Taking the watch off works too.

How do I pause my streak?

On days you're excused from prayer, go to More › Pause my streak (or tap your streak on the Namaz screen) and turn it on. Paused days don't count and don't break your streak. Tap Streak paused at the top of Today to resume.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Tahir and choose the small or medium size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize and add Tahir there.

The Hijri date is a day out from my mosque

Tahir works out the Hijri date by calculation, and your mosque may follow the sighting of the new moon. Go to More › Preferences › Hijri date and move it a day either way.

Does Tahir work offline?

Yes. Tahir saves the next two weeks of prayer times on your phone, so times, reminders and widgets keep working without a connection. It refreshes them when you're back online.

The times are for the wrong place

Tahir needs location access to know where you are. Allow it in iOS Settings › Apps › Tahir › Location ("While Using the App" is enough), then open Tahir. If you've travelled, opening the app updates your times for the new place.

How do I use the Qibla compass?

Hold your phone flat and away from metal, magnets and speakers. If the direction seems off, move your phone in a figure of eight to recalibrate the compass. The Qibla direction is worked out on your phone from your location.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Tahir stores is kept on your phone, so deleting the app deletes all of it. We don't have a copy.
