Test Tahir on Android

Google needs 12 people to test a new app for 14 days before it can go public. Three quick steps on your Android phone and you're one of them. JazakAllah khair.

You're on an iPhone or iPad. Tahir is already on the App Store for you. These steps are for Android phones, so send this page to anyone with one.

Download on the App Store

Open this page on your Android phone. Every step needs the Google account that's on your phone: tahir5dailyprayers.com/android

  1. 1

    Join the tester list

    Tap Join group on the page that opens. It then says "No conversations yet": that's fine, come back here.

    Join the tester list
  2. 2

    Become a tester

    Wait a couple of minutes after step 1, then tap below and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available yet, Google hasn't caught up: try again in 10 minutes. If it says you're already a tester, you're in.

    Become a tester
  3. 3

    Install Tahir from Google Play

    Once you're a tester, this opens Tahir in the Play Store. Tap Install. If it says "Item not found", Google can take up to an hour to catch up: see below.

    Open in Google Play
  4. 4

    Keep it for 14 days

    Open Tahir, allow your location (for your prayer times) and notifications (for the adhan), and use it for your prayers. That's all Google needs.

If something isn't working

Google Play says "Item not found" or "not available"

Do the steps in order: Google Play only shows Tahir after step 2. Then check these:

  • Give it time. After you become a tester, Google Play can take anywhere from a few minutes to an hour to show Tahir.
  • Check the account. In the Play Store, tap You at the bottom (or your picture at the top right) to see which account it's using. It has to be the one that joined in step 1. Switch to it, or do steps 1 and 2 again with it.
  • Clear the Play Store's cache. Go to your phone's Settings › Apps › Google Play Store › Storage and tap Clear cache, then tap step 3 again.
A Google sign-in page opened instead

The link opened inside WhatsApp, Instagram or another app, which isn't signed in to Google. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose Open in Chrome, or copy the link into Chrome.

I have more than one Google account

Use the one your Play Store uses, for every step. On the tester-list page and the Become a tester page, tap your picture at the top right to switch accounts first.

I already have the Tahir APK

Install from Google Play anyway. It updates the app you have and keeps your streak and history.

Is it free? What about my data?

Yes, it's free, with no ads and no sign-up. Your prayer history stays on your phone. See our privacy policy.
