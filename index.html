Five prayers. One sky.

Your five daily prayers, under a sky that moves with the day.

Tahir is a calm prayer companion for iPhone and Apple Watch: accurate prayer times, the adhan, the Qibla, and simple tracking for the five daily prayers and Tahajjud. No ads, no accounts and nothing to distract you.

The sky clock

A sky that follows your day

Tahir's sky moves with the sun: indigo at Fajr, bright blue at Dhuhr, gold at Asr, red at Maghrib and starry after Isha. One glance tells you where you are in the day and how long until the next prayer.

Everything you need

Simple tools for a steady practice

Prayer times your way

Accurate times wherever you are. Tahir standard follows alislam.org, or choose from 22 other recognised methods, including Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi and Diyanet, or set your own. Standard or Hanafi Asr.

The adhan at every prayer

A reminder when each prayer begins, with the adhan, a soft chime or nothing, chosen prayer by prayer. A gentle "Did you pray?" follow-up lets you tick it off right from the notification.

Track the five prayers

Tick off each prayer as you pray it. Your streak, your last seven days and a full history help you stay consistent, gently.

Streaks and medals

Earn medals for days in a row with all five prayers, from three days to a year, and for nights of Tahajjud. On days you're excused from prayer, pause your streak so it isn't broken.

A dua and tasbeeh after prayer

When you tick off a prayer, Tahir shows a dua from the Quran and Sunnah, in Arabic with its meaning and source, and counts your tasbeeh: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu Akbar.

Before time runs out

Between Fajr and sunrise, and between Asr and sunset, Tahir counts down to sunrise or sunset, so you can pray in good time.

Tahajjud

See when the last third of the night begins, set a quiet reminder and keep track of the nights you rise.

Qibla compass

Hold your phone flat and turn until it says you're facing the Qibla, with the distance to Makkah. It's worked out on your phone.

Rakats and timetable

How many rakats each prayer has, including Jummah and the shortened prayers when you travel, and the whole month's prayer times with the Hijri date.

Learn and reflect

The 99 Names of Allah, a hadith each day, stories of the Prophets and the Sahaba, and tonight's moon.

Home Screen widgets

The next prayer and today's times on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and in StandBy, on the same sky as the app.

Apple Watch

The next prayer, today's prayers to tick off, the Qibla, the tasbeeh with the Digital Crown, your week and your medals, on your wrist, with complications for your watch face.

Works offline

The next two weeks of prayer times are saved on your phone, so times, reminders and widgets keep working without a connection.

Private by design

Your worship stays between you and Allah

Tahir has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your prayer history is stored only on your phone. Only your approximate location is used, to work out your prayer times. Read the privacy policy.
