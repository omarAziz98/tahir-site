Five prayers. One sky.

Your five daily prayers, under a sky that moves with the day.

Tahir is a calm prayer companion for iPhone, with accurate prayer times, gentle reminders and simple tracking for the five daily prayers and Tahajjud. No ads, no accounts and nothing to distract you.

Coming soon to the App Store

The sky clock

A sky that follows your day

Tahir's sky moves with the sun: indigo at Fajr, bright blue at Dhuhr, gold at Asr, red at Maghrib and starry after Isha. One glance tells you where you are in the day and how long until the next prayer.

Everything you need

Simple tools for a steady practice

Prayer times your way

Accurate times wherever you are. Choose from 14 recognised calculation methods, including Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi and Diyanet, or set your own. Standard or Hanafi Asr.

Track the five prayers

Tick off each prayer as you pray it. Your streak, your last seven days and a full history help you stay consistent, gently.

Reminders that arrive

A reminder before each prayer and a gentle follow-up if you haven't ticked it off. They're set a week ahead on your phone, so they arrive even when you're offline.

A dua after each prayer

When you tick off a prayer, Tahir shows a short dua from the Sunnah to recite before you get on with your day.

Tahajjud

See when the last third of the night begins, set a quiet reminder and keep track of the nights you rise.

Qibla compass

Find the direction of the Kaabah from anywhere. It's worked out on your phone.

Works offline

The next two weeks of prayer times are saved on your phone, so times, reminders and widgets keep working without a connection.

Learn

The 99 Names of Allah, a daily hadith, and stories of the Prophets and the Sahaba.

Home Screen widgets

The next prayer and today's times on your Home Screen, on the same sky as the app.

Private by design

Your worship stays between you and Allah

Tahir has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your prayer history is stored only on your phone. Only your approximate location is used, to work out your prayer times. Read the privacy policy.
